# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the Bedriox organization owners. Enforcement decisions are made by Bedriox maintainers in good faith and may include warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal.
